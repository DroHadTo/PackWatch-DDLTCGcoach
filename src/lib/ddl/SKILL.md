# Pack Watch — turn playbook

Advice only. The human clicks. Never inject into ddltcg.com. Never automate input.

Read the board fresh every turn. Do not trust memorised card text over printed text.

## Every beat, in this order

1. Surface a past misplay that matches this board (Taunt, unspent mana, trap timing, clogged lanes, bad trade).
2. Lethal: if face is open and the math finishes the Hero, take it.
3. Taunt lock: crack Taunt before face or non-Taunt.
4. Kill-gate: creature fights only if someone dies (ATK ≥ HP, Poison, or a stack). No chip on bodies.
5. Rush: bodies this turn, never the Hero this turn.
6. Traps: cannot spring the turn they are set.
7. Spend mana. It does not bank. Coin is +1 on one play, not a cap raise.
8. Lanes: five slots, one body each, empty stays empty — place on purpose.
9. Class plan: Crown walls, Bow chains, Wizard answers, Zombie grave, Pirate tempo.

Keep advice to one or two sentences plus the recommended action.

## Five bleeds

- Over-extending into Taunt
- Ignoring the mana curve (unspent crystals)
- Missing trap answers / springing too early
- Poor lane management
- Bad trades (illegal chip, suicide for nothing)

## Facts

40-card decks, max 3 copies. 40 Hero HP. Five creature lanes. Five back-row. Mana 1→10, no bank. Draw 4, bounce 5-cost openers. Second player Coin. Hand cap 10. Fatigue 1,2,3… Wayne (V029) banned online.
